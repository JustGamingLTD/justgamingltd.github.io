<p>An all-online version of the classic word game hangman for you to play with friends, family, or anyone else!</p>

<p><b>Play Anywhere, With Anyone</b> – You can play Multiplayer Hangman 2 anywhere you have your phone. Play it at home with your family, with your friends while waiting for the bus, or even completely remotely!</p>

<p><b>Horn Alerts</b> – Is someone not paying attention? You can send them a fun horn sound to get them to focus again.</p>

<p><b>Strategic Word-Picking</b> – You choose which words your fellow players will have to guess. Will you pick a short word with rare letters, or a long difficult word? The choice is up to you.</p>

<p><b>Scoreboard</b> – You can track who is doing well in your game by simply looking at the scoreboard. Who will be the first to score 10 points?</p>

<p><b>Word of the Day</b> – Each day a new word for you to solve appears at midnight. Play at your own pace, compete against others’ scores, and solve the puzzle to save the hangman. Track your scores, and return tomorrow for a fresh word.</p>

<p><b>Cross-Platform Play</b> – You can even play Multiplayer Hangman 2 with your friends that don't have an Android device. The web-version can be played on any device, even on iOS!</p>
