Multiplayer Hangman 2 does not collect any personal information. To facilitate online multiplayer, your username, the words you pick, and the letters you guess are transmitted over a secure connection to a remote server operated by the developer. This data is then forwarded to the other players in your game. The data is only processed transiently and not logged.

The app displays advertisements from Google AdMob. These ads are not personalized. Therefore, AdMob collects only device and usage data such as device model, OS version, app‑instance ID, IP address (from which your general location can be inferred), and ad‑request timestamps, to serve non‑personalized ads and report ad performance. This data is not used to build a user profile. The ad_id permission is requested by the Google AdMob SDK, but its usage is disabled.

For details on Google’s data collection, see Google’s privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy]
You can manage your ad preferences on Google’s Ad Settings page: [https://adsettings.google.com/]

Multiplayer Hangman is developed by Freya Winters. For enquiries, contact support [at] justgamingltd [dot] nl.
