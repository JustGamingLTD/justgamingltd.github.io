Multiplayer Hangman 2 does not collect any personal information. To facilitate online multiplayer, your username, the words you pick, and the letters you guess are transmitted over a secure connection to a remote server operated by the developer. This data is then forwarded to the other players in your game. The data is only processed transiently and not logged. Only very basic information, such as the total number of players playing the game at some time and how many people complete the word of the day are logged.

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, location data (derived from IP), advertising ID, and usage information such as app version, session duration, and interaction with ads. This information is transmitted to Google’s servers over a secure connection and is used for ad personalization, frequency capping, fraud detection, and reporting. The data is retained by Google in accordance with its own privacy policies and is not stored or logged by our app beyond the transmission required for ad delivery. For users located in the European Economic Area, we treat the absence of an explicit consent signal as a refusal to consent; therefore, no personal data required for personalized advertising is processed for those users.

For details on Google’s data collection, see Google’s privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
You can manage your ad preferences on Google’s Ad Settings page: <https://adsettings.google.com/>
